Electricians Singapore homes and businesses rely on

Founded in 2011, Ren X Electrical has grown from a two-man outfit into a trusted team of licensed electricians serving the whole of Singapore.

Our story

Built on referrals, not ads

Ren X started in a single Ang Mo Kio shophouse with one van and a promise: show up on time, quote honestly, and do the job properly the first time. Fifteen years on, that promise hasn't changed — even as we've grown to serve HDB estates, condos, offices and factories island-wide.

Today our team of EMA-licensed electricians handles everything from a single faulty switch to full commercial rewiring projects, still run with the same hands-on attention as job number one.
